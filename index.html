Operating Specifications & Guidelines

Lab Break Rules & Unattended Belongings

Clear, community-agreed procedures for stepping away, handling unattended belongings, and fair workstation sharing.

01 — What to Do When Belongings Are Unattended

Situation A: No Active Break — Blank Status

Status: BLANK

If the computer is physically unattended, carefully move belongings to a safe spot beside the same workstation and use the computer.

  • Careful & respectful handling: Students may move unattended items to a safe location immediately beside that workstation (e.g., side of the desk or floor beside the same chair).
  • Strict boundaries: Do not throw, damage, open, search, take away, or unnecessarily handle anyone's belongings.
  • Keep items together: Belongings must remain together and visible in a safe location beside the same seat.
  • Prevent computer hoarding: Leaving belongings behind does not informally reserve a computer. Students may use the workstation immediately.

Situation B: Active Break — On Break

Status: ON BREAK (15:00)

Do not use the computer. Wait until the 15-minute countdown expires.

  • Active break priority: An active break timer takes priority over the physical absence of the student.
  • Full entitlement: The student who initiated the break is entitled to the full 15-minute break period under these guidelines.
  • Student return: If the student returns before the timer reaches 00:00, the computer remains theirs to use.
  • Timer expiry: If the 15-minute countdown concludes and the seat remains physically unattended, follow the Blank Status instructions above.
Important Occupancy Clarification
A Blank status does not automatically prove physical vacancy; it strictly confirms that no active break timer exists. The website does not monitor physical occupancy. Students must exercise reasonable judgement and follow college lab policies. This guidance does not represent an official administrative policy or authorize the seizure of personal property. If ownership or occupancy is unclear, please seek assistance from authorized lab staff rather than risk a dispute.

02 — How The System Works

  1. 1. The dashboard displays live 15-minute break timers and cooldown status across CCC and IDC workstations.
  2. 2. Before stepping away, sign in with your official @iisertvm.ac.in account and activate your 15-minute break timer.
  3. 3. The workstation displays an amber active break countdown visible to everyone in the lab.
  4. 4. Return before the 15-minute timer expires and click "I'm Back" to conclude your break.
  5. 5. When the timer expires, the computer automatically returns to Blank status and enters a 15-minute workstation cooldown.
  6. 6. A blank workstation means no active break is running. Unattended belongings may be set aside respectfully.

03 — Community Rules & Cooldowns

  • Rule 1 — 15-minute fixed break limit Every break is strictly limited to 15 minutes. Extensions are not permitted.
  • Rule 2 — Account cooldown (60 minutes) Each student account can start at most one break within a rolling 60-minute window across both labs.
  • Rule 3 — Computer cooldown (15 minutes) When a computer's break timer expires, that workstation enters a 15-minute cooldown where no new break timer can be started on it.
  • Rule 4 — One active computer per account An account can only hold one computer on break at a time across both CCC and IDC facilities.
  • Rule 5 — No informal computer reservation Leaving belongings unattended on a computer without an active break timer does not reserve it.
  • Rule 6 — Automatic return to Blank Once the countdown concludes, the workstation returns to Blank. Other students may then use it per lab guidelines.
04 — Account-Based Usage & Dual Cooldown Policy

Break timers are linked directly to your authenticated Google account. You do not need to enter a roll number to start a break.

Dual Cooldown System: Two separate restrictions are enforced simultaneously at the database level:
• Account-Level: You may activate one break every rolling 60 minutes.
• Computer-Level: Each workstation enters a 15-minute cooldown period following break expiry, during which no user can start another break on that computer.

Privacy Statement: The application uses your authenticated account identity solely to enforce fair usage. Only the minimal information necessary to operate the break timer is processed. Your email address is strictly confidential and is never displayed publicly on the computer-status dashboard.

05 — Student Welfare & Administrative Notice

This website is an independently developed utility provided to encourage fair and responsible use of computer lab facilities in consultation with Student Welfare. The rules displayed here are community guidelines. Students are expected to respect active break countdowns and follow these guidelines in good faith.

If someone does not follow the rules, leaves belongings without activating a timer, or interferes with another student's belongings, please inform the lab attendants or the Student Welfare office. Please use the system responsibly, respect fellow students, and follow all official institute lab policies.
