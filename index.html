Guidelines & Instructions

Lab Break Rules & How It Works

A fair-use community system for students stepping away from their computer desks for short breaks.

How the system works

  1. 1 The website is designed to display active break timers for computers in the lab.
  2. 2 If you need to step away temporarily, select your computer and activate the 15-minute break timer.
  3. 3 Your computer will display an active break countdown.
  4. 4 Return before the timer expires.
  5. 5 Once the timer expires, the computer automatically returns to its blank state.
  6. 6 A blank computer tile means that no active break timer exists. It does not confirm whether someone is physically occupying the computer.

Community Rules

  • Rule 1 — 15-minute break limit
    Every break is limited to 15 minutes. No extensions are permitted.
  • Rule 2 — One break per hour
    Each roll number can activate only one break within a rolling 60-minute period.
  • Rule 3 — No computer reservation
    This website is NOT a computer reservation system. You cannot reserve a computer by leaving your belongings on it.
  • Rule 4 — Activate the timer before leaving
    If you leave your computer without activating the break timer, the computer has no active digital break protection.
  • Rule 5 — Timer expiry
    Once the timer expires, the computer automatically returns to its blank state. Other students may then use the computer in accordance with the lab rules.
  • Rule 6 — Follow the rules
    Students are expected to respect the system and one another.

Important Notice

This website is an independently developed utility intended to encourage fair and responsible use of computer lab facilities. The rules displayed here are community guidelines and are not officially enforced by the college administration. Students are expected to respect the break timers and follow the guidelines.

If someone does not follow the rules, leaves belongings without activating a timer, or interferes with another student's belongings, the website developers cannot guarantee any intervention, recovery or resolution. Please use the system responsibly, respect other students and follow the applicable college lab policies. The system is provided as a convenience and should not be treated as an official administrative authority.
